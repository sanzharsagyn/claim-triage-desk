Claim Triage Desk

A tool for life insurance claims. An LLM reads Czech, Ukrainian and Russian claim documents and has to quote the exact source of every fact. Code verifies each quote, checks the policy terms and calculates the payout. A claims handler makes the decision, and overrides of the AI are logged.

  • Runs live on Claude Haiku 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 through its own API, with caching, per-visitor limits and a daily budget.
  • Rejects any fact whose quote is not in the document, any impossible date and any invalid ICD-10 code.
  • Catches instructions hidden in documents, such as white text in an invoice telling the AI to approve the claim.
  • Measured on a test set of 24 documents with reference labels, comparing a cheap and a strong model.

Úřadomat: the problem

Thousands of foreigners in Czechia run a small business as OSVČ and have to extend their residence regularly. Many applications run into trouble for reasons that are easy to avoid:

  • They bring a tax return (daňové přiznání) when the Ministry expects the tax office's assessment (platební výměr).
  • A no-debt certificate is missing. There are four offices, and each certificate must be less than 30 days old on the day you apply.
  • Their income is below the legal minimum: the household's subsistence minimum plus housing costs. Almost nobody knows that number for their family.

Try the core calculation

This is the same rule the site uses (§ 46 odst. 7 of Act No. 326/1999 Sb.): subsistence minimum of the household plus standard housing costs, taken at the upper limit published on ipc.gov.cz.

Subsistence minimum (životní minimum)
Standard rent + energy allowance
Required monthly income

Outside Prague this is an upper limit. The full site also compares it with your actual rent and with your income from the tax assessment: (tax base − tax − insurance) / 12. An immigration lawyer checked the method on 12 test cases, and all 12 matched practice.

Build log

Each version came from feedback by someone who knows the domain or uses the tool.

  1. 12 Octv8

    First public version. It asked for name and passport number and had an AI write a cover letter to the Ministry.

  2. 13 Octv9

    Two legal advisers working with foreigners reviewed it. AI cover letter: plays no role in the procedure. Name and passport fields: no purpose, so no reason to collect them under GDPR. Both removed, along with the AI backend. No-debt certificates now appear only where the law requires them. Ukrainian added.

  3. 14 Octv10–v12

    Tax assessment vs. tax return, separate rules for the first business year and the flat-rate tax. The income calculator, then actual housing costs with service charges, then the lawyer's income formula.

  4. 15 Octv13

    12 control cases sent to a lawyer, 12 confirmed. New hint: in a household with two adults, the second income can be counted too.

  5. 24 Octv14–v15

    A user in a developer community chat saw the date field as mm/dd/yyyy. Replaced the browser date picker with day, month and year lists in the site's language.

  6. 27 Octv16–v17

    Paid add-on removed: the tool is free for good. English interface added. Sent to eight regional integration centres for foreigners.
